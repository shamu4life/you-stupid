The Plush

Nothing beats a good burger and we’ve got the best cook on the grill! Uwoslab’s back at Youtooz with a perfectly juicy summer plush! Sitting at 9 inches tall with his legs kicked forward and dressed in two-tone brown shoes, khaki pants, a blue t-shirt and flame-printed apron that says “spicy Boi” on the chest pocket, he holds a large spatula in his right hand as black shades rest over blushing cheeks and an open mouth while a white chef’s hat rests on his grey hair. This plush is packed to the seams with 100% PP cotton and is made of the minky and tricot materials with accents of felt, perfect for cuddling or flipping burgers with!

About UwosLab

UwosLab is a mad scientist, Twitch streamer and YouTuber! Bringing the weirdest, wildest and absolutely bonkers creations to life with the power of science, his (completely safe) DIY projects have electrified his nearly 436k YouTube subscribers and 94k Twitch followers!
